About
Chris Sanchez
I’m currently a full time student studying a bachelor of design at Swinburne Online with a major in communication design. Over the course of my studies I have received both a letter of commendation from Samar Zutshi, The Academic Director of Swinburne Online and a Career Development Certificate. Determination is a personal strength of high value and this is reflected in my achievements and ability to always produce a finished product to a professional standard.
I love finding inspiration in nature and using that in my art - working with different mediums has greatly benefitted my approach to design and my ability to conceptualise, develop and refine projects. On the flipside I love sport and particularly Formula One and currently testing myself against some of the best in virtual sim-racing competition.
