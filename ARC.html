Capturing the war memorial within a symbol was difficult as there are many sensitive factors to be considered. The premise of the design was to work with the poem by John McCrae In Flanders Fields, particularly focusing on the imagery in first line through the use of poppies scattered between the crosses.
Colour Variations
These variations show the flexiblity of the symbol. Working to make the poppies visable in the black and white variation was a challenge however I think the result is effective.
