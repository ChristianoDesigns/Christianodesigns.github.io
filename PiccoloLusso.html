  • Piccolo Lusso
Packaging design for a coppola hat and driving gloves.

Client:
Fiat

Project:
Piccolo Lusso

Year:
2021

Type:
Packaging

Colours:
Pantone
This design was for a coppola hat and driving gloves with the idea of evoking a journey. The packaging was made to resemble an Italian street sign while the visual elements show architecture from the city of Turin. The box sleeve opens to reveal two separate containers for the items.
Inner Packaging
On the base of the containers are blurbs about the respective items. Additionally hand drawn sketches makes the packaging feel more authentic.
Dieline
There are several elements to this packaging which creates an interesting progression as the consumer opens their item. The coppola hat and gloves are in seperate sections however join together to form a single box which then fits inside the sleeve.
